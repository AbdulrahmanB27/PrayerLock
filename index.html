More ways
to stay connected.

Find your direction.

Swipe between your timetable, Qibla and Adhkar. The Qibla compass shows your heading and the direction of prayer, with a guided calibration animation.

Keep remembrance close.

Morning, evening, after prayer, and before sleep. Count each repetition with a tap and choose from six Arabic fonts. In English, PrayerLock remembers your choice to show or hide transliteration and translation. Open Hadith details and tap a citation to copy it.

A rhythm that’s yours.

Choose focus windows and optional reminders before Adhan. Set Tasbeeh groups and repetition targets, with touch feedback you can turn on or off.
