Privacy policy.

PrayerLock for Android
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

This is the privacy policy provided in the PrayerLock app. For questions about this policy, contact prayerlockapp@gmail.com.

1. Data Collection and Usage

PrayerLock is designed to be privacy-first. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, usage stats, or tracking metrics off your device. All functions, including the calculation of prayer times, lockout timers, and geographical adjustments, are performed entirely locally on your phone.

2. Permissions

To function as intended, PrayerLock requires the following permissions:

  • Location Permissions: Used strictly to auto-detect your timezone and calculate accurate local prayer times. Location data is never shared.
  • Accessibility Service & Draw Over Other Apps: Used exclusively to enforce the focus lockout screen over active applications during prayer times, and to navigate back to the home screen if bypassed. We do not monitor your keystrokes, screen contents, or history through this service.
  • Notifications: Used to remind you of upcoming active lockout periods.

3. Offline First

All your preferences are saved locally using Android's built-in SharedPreferences system. Since there is no remote server to sync to, your data remains yours and yours alone.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect app updates and features. Any updates will be available within the app.
