Privacy policy.

PrayerLock for Android
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Updated November 9, 2026

This policy describes how the PrayerLock Android app handles information. For questions about this policy, contact prayerlockapp@gmail.com.

1. Data Collection and Usage

PrayerLock calculates prayer times and Qibla direction on your device. It uses your settings to show your timetable, provide reminders, and personalize features such as Adhkar and focus sessions. You can use PrayerLock without an account.

2. Device Access and Services

PrayerLock uses Android permissions and services for the following features.

  • Location and geocoding. If you grant location access, PrayerLock can use your device location for prayer times, Qibla, and optional saved zones. You can also enter coordinates manually. When you search for a city or Android looks up a place name for coordinates, Android’s Geocoder service may send the search text or coordinates to its network geocoding providers.
  • Display over other apps. With your Android permission, PrayerLock can display its prayer or focus screen over another app. A foreground service maintains the screen during the session and posts an ongoing notification. The overlay displays PrayerLock’s own interface without reading other apps’ screen content or recording keystrokes.
  • App detection. If you enable Android Usage Access, PrayerLock reads recent app usage events to identify the app in the foreground and decide when to show or hide its prayer or focus overlay. App detection is processed on your device.
  • App selection. PrayerLock lists apps with a launcher entry when you open settings that ask you to select apps. Your selections are saved in the app’s local preferences.
  • Notifications and audio. PrayerLock uses Android alarms and notifications for reminders and can play your selected Adhan audio. The app uses exact alarms for scheduled prayers and focus blocks when Android allows them. Delivery depends on device settings and Android scheduling. Optional Masjid mode can change your ringer mode. If you select a custom audio file, Android can grant access to that file for later playback.
  • NFC and clipboard. If an NFC tag is linked, PrayerLock uses its identifier for check-in. If you tap a Hadith citation to copy it, PrayerLock places the citation on the Android clipboard.

3. Storage, Backup and Deletion

PrayerLock stores schedules, coordinates and saved zones, app selections, linked tag identifiers, references to selected audio, and Adhkar settings and completion state in private app storage so they are available between sessions. The current Android backup rules exclude PrayerLock’s main preferences and its Adhkar preferences from backup and device transfer. Android can handle other eligible app data according to your device and backup settings. To remove local PrayerLock data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings. This also resets your preferences.

4. Website, Alpha Testing and Support

This website serves static pages without advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub describes its handling of this information in its privacy statement.

Joining the alpha uses Google Groups and Google Play. Your Google account, group membership and any messages you post are handled through those services under Google’s privacy policy. Group administrators can access membership information made available by Google Groups to manage tester eligibility and feedback. You can leave the group or manage your posts in Google Groups. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. For questions or requests about information you share with PrayerLock, contact the support address below.

We retain any tester records we hold only while needed to manage test access and feedback, and support correspondence only while needed to resolve your request and related follow-up. We delete records when those purposes end, unless a legal obligation requires retention. You can request deletion through the support address below. Google manages records held by its services under its own policies and account controls.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as PrayerLock changes. The latest version is available on this page, linked from the app.
